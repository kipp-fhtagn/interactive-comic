IN JUST A FEW SECONDS, WE REALIZED THAT THE HOUSE WAS COLLAPSING ON US, WITH THE WALLS FALLING AROUND US.

THE BED HAD FALLEN ON THE CHILD’S HEAD.

HIS HAND WAS AMPUTATED AND LEFT BESIDE HIM.

HIS OTHER HAND WAS SHATTERED, IN ANOTHER ROOM.

OH, GOD! HIS FACE WAS DEVASTATED.
